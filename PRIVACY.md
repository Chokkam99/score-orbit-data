# ScoreOrbit privacy policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

ScoreOrbit is an Android app by Chokkam Labs that shows results for India's and Singapore's badminton and table tennis players. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## What we collect

Nothing. ScoreOrbit has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not collect, store or share any personal data, and it does not send anything about you to us.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves these on your phone only:

- the country you chose (India or Singapore)
- your sports filter
- your theme choice (light, dark or the system setting)
- the last copy of the public results and schedule files, and when they were last downloaded, so the app works offline

None of this leaves your phone, and it is not included in Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it all.

## What the app downloads

To show current results, the app downloads two public data files (`results.json` and `upcoming.json`) from GitHub, at `raw.githubusercontent.com`. It does this when you open the app, when you come back to it after six hours or more, and when you pull to refresh or tap Sync now. These are plain downloads over HTTPS. The app sends nothing about you with them: no account, no identifier, no location and no usage data.

Like any web request, each download lets GitHub see standard request data: your IP address, the time, and your phone's standard Android user agent (which names the Android version and phone model). GitHub handles that data under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Links to other sites

Each tournament lists the public pages its data came from, such as Wikipedia. If you tap one, it opens in your web browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

ScoreOrbit is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
